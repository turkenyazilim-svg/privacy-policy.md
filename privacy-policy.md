📄 Document Data & On-Device Processing

Documents you create or manage (such as PDFs, scans, signatures, and related files) are stored locally on your device.

The Application does not upload, access, or store your document contents on external servers by default.
The Application does not read or analyze your documents beyond what is necessary to provide its features.

Thumbnails and temporary working files are also kept on your device; they are used to display pages and to produce export results.

When you choose to export or share a document, the destination app or service you pick (for example WhatsApp, email, or your file manager) handles that content under its own privacy policy.

🧠 OCR & Processing

Some features (such as text recognition) use on-device technologies like Google ML Kit.

OCR processing is performed locally on your device
Your document data is not transmitted to external servers for OCR processing

The Application may create local logs for troubleshooting, and — after consent where consent is required — send crash and stability reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics.
